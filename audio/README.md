# Audio

Clips a style can use, picked by name in the Styles tab:

- **Opening card** and **Closing card** each take a clip, played over that card.
  Longer than the card and it is trimmed; shorter and the rest is silence.
- **Music** plays under the whole video at a low volume, looped to reach the
  end and faded in and out.

Drop `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.ogg`, `.opus`, `.aac` or `.flac` in here and it
turns up in the dropdowns. The file name becomes the label, so
`brand-sting.mp3` reads as "Brand sting".

Nothing ships with the project: music and stings are licensed per use, and
guessing wrong on somebody's behalf is not a favour. Whatever you put here
travels with the project, so check the licence covers the videos you make.
