Walkthrough Recorder

Turn a page on your site into a narrated video.

Paste the address of the page where the walkthrough should start.
  1. A browser window opens with your site in it.
  2. Use the site as you normally would. Every click is written down.
  3. Type what you want said out loud in the panel on the right.
  4. Press Save flow when you are finished.
Recording. Use the other window.
  1. Click through your site the way you would show it to someone.
  2. Fill in the narration and hints in the panel on the right.
  3. Press Save flow to come back here.
Making your video. This takes a minute or two.
Your video is ready.
